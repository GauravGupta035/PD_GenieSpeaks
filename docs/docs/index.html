  • Problem Statement / EPIC

    While searching for a product to purchase, we often rely on reviews for it found on different websites to know about the experiences of people before purchasing it, especially in cases of larger monetary investments. If history is evident, there have been multiple cases where “too-good-to-be-true” deals have blinded customers to make purchases that ended up as a scam.

    Our Solution

    To provide customers with real reviews for products, we aggregate reviews for the requested product from multiple authentic websites and present it to the customers in a single location. Apart from providing just product reviews, our objective is also to tackle the problem of “too-good-to-be-true” deals, by aggregating and providing reviews of the organization producing the product. Using this method, customers will find it easier to know about the product as well as the organization producing that product and encourage conscious decision-making.

    We plan to achieve this goal by scraping reviews from various authentic websites and displaying them in a single location, our website. We will automate the process of gathering reviews using Python. We plan to build our website using the MERN stack (MongoDB, ExpressJS, ReactJS, Node) and host the Node and Python applications on Azure Cloud.

    About Us

    GenieSpeaks is a one-stop shop for reviews for all varities of products aggregated from websites like Amazon and Flipkart. You can search for the product you need to know about and get reviews about them from various website at a single location as well as provide you with the best deal out of all these E-commerce websites.

    Landing Page

    In the landing page you can either directly search for the product of your want or sign in.

    PS: We also have a dark mode!

    Search Page

    You can search for the product of your choice and filter according to various parameters like Brand, Price Range, etc.

    Product Details

    This is where all the reviews and ratings for your selected products are displayed. All the reviews are collected from Amazon and Flipkart (More websites joining soon).
